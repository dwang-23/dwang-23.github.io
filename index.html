About

Hi, there! It's nice to meet you.

My name is Daniel Wang, and I'm a first-year marketing Ph.D. student at the Tepper School of Business at Carnegie Mellon University. I am interested in studying the applications of AI in marketing and developing quantitative models of consumer behavior on digital platforms.

Before, I earned a B.S. in Computer Science from the University of Maryland, College Park. Previously, I have worked on projects studying digital twins in marketing, recommender systems' impact on customer lifetime value, and large language model biases.

In my free time, I enjoy playing guitar, doing photography, and being outdoors.
